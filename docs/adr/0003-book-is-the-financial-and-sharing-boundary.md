# Book is the financial and sharing boundary

A `Book` is a self-contained financial dataset that owns its base currency, accounts, transactions, categories, counterparties, reserves, and history; installations may contain multiple Books, and future invitations grant access to one Book. `Book` replaces `Household` because membership belongs at the shared-data boundary, and it is preferred over YNAB's `Budget` term to avoid conflating the dataset with periodic spending budgets.
